# Codex prompt for the robot head

Run from an empty directory: `codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -s workspace-write "<prompt>"`.

> Use your image generation tool to create 3 different candidate images (separate files
> head-1.png, head-2.png, head-3.png in the current directory), each 1024x1024 PNG with a fully
> transparent background.
>
> Subject: a cute, friendly robot HEAD only — no neck, no body, no arms — seen straight from the
> front, centered, filling ~85% of the canvas. Rounded helmet shape in glossy white/pearl with soft
> studio shading, a big dark navy glass visor (face screen) covering the front, and round
> headphone-style ear pods on both sides with glowing cyan/electric-blue accent rings
> (#00BFFF / #29FFD0). Small antenna or light detail on top is fine.
>
> CRITICAL: the visor must be completely EMPTY — no eyes, no mouth, no face, no text, no
> reflections shaped like eyes. Just a smooth, evenly dark navy (#020c1e .. #04152e) glass surface
> with at most a subtle soft highlight near the top edge. Eyes will be drawn on top later in code.
>
> Style: modern 3D render, clean, premium, toy-like (like a desk companion product mockup), neon
> sci-fi accents, no outer glow or halo around the head, no drop shadow, no background, no frame.
> It will be shown small (~170 px) on a dark round display, so keep shapes bold and readable.
